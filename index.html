Show Goal-Directed Perfusion Reference Basis SOP p.21, 2025

1. Oxygen Content

Arterial O₂ Content (CaO₂) = (1.34 × Hb × SaO₂) + (0.003 × PaO₂)

Venous O₂ Content (CvO₂) = (1.34 × Hb × SvO₂) + (0.003 × PvO₂)

Target value of 17–20 mL/dL

2. Oxygen Consumption

The difference between the oxygen concentration delivered to the tissues and what is left over on the venous side per unit time.

VO₂ = CI × (CaO₂ - CvO₂) × 10

Target value of 110–160 mLO₂/min/m²

3. Oxygen Delivery

It is the product of the amount of Oxygen delivered per unit time.

DO₂ = CI × ((1.34 × Hgb × Oxygen Saturation) + (paO₂ × 0.003)) × 10

Adult Target Value: 280 mLO₂/min/m² | Pediatric and Neonate Target Value: 340 mLO₂/min/m²

4. Oxygen Extraction

It is the ratio (percentage) of Oxygen concentration present and what is used.

ER% = VO₂ / DO₂

*The rate of Oxygen extraction will vary according to the needs of the tissues. If blood flow drops off, extraction rate should increase as a compensatory mechanism. Likewise, if the metabolic demand increases, extraction rate will increase.
